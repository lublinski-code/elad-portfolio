# The problem

A high-value segment of creators was structurally blocked from receiving sponsorship offers because their representation lived in a different workflow than our marketplace assumed.

## The approach

Mapped the existing agency workflow end to end. Found the seam where the marketplace could plug in without forcing agencies to change how they worked.

## The outcome

2.4x more campaigns per creator in the affected segment.
