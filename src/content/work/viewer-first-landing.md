# The moment

The viewer was at the peak of intent — they had just seen the creator's pitch — and the next step in our funnel was a generic page that didn't match the energy.

## The fix

Built a viewer-first landing surface that opened with the creator's offer and matched the visual context the viewer arrived from.

## The outcome

A meaningful lift in conversion at the moment that mattered most.
