# The diagnosis

The core game loop was solo. Players had no reason to come back if their friends weren't there. Retention was a flat line.

## The bet

Introduce social mechanics that give the loop a second pull: groups and one-on-one duels.

## The result

2.4x more campaigns per creator and a measurable lift in D7 retention.
