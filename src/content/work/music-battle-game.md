# The question I started with

Could a solo founder operate with the decision-making support of a full C-suite and the execution support of a full engineering team, using AI as the connective tissue?

## What I shipped

A live music battle game, built from zero in three weeks. Real users. Real game loop. Real deploy pipeline.

## What I learned

The quality of what AI produces is almost always a reflection of how clearly you understood the problem going in. That's where the design thinking still lives.
