# The signal

Players were dropping off the platform 80% within days of a campaign ending. The product was working as designed and still failing.

## The reframe

The user we were optimizing for wasn't the only user that mattered. The audience watching the campaign had its own job-to-be-done, and we hadn't been designing for it.

## The result

+21% ROAS at D30 once the audience-first surfaces shipped.
