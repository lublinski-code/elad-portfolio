# The hypothesis

The product had a content engine but no rhythm. We thought a smarter notification cadence and a live ops layer could pull players back into the habit loop.

## The work

Mapped every existing trigger, designed a tiered notification system, and prototyped a live ops surface.

## What happened

It didn't ship. Priorities shifted. The artifact still exists and the thinking still holds.
